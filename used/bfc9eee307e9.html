Experience the perfect blend of luxury, performance, and advanced technology with the 2019 Ford Explorer Platinum, designed for confident driving in any condition.

This Blue Metallic 2019 Ford Explorer Platinum is powered by a robust 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with a 6-speed SelectShift automatic transmission featuring paddle shifters, delivering responsive and smooth performance. Its 4x4 drivetrain ensures excellent traction and control, ideal for Canadian roads and weather. The exterior is enhanced by 20-inch aluminum wheels, LED headlights, fog lamps, and a twin panel moonroof that fills the cabin with natural light.

Inside, the Ebony Leather interior offers heated and cooled front seats with power adjustments, lumbar support, and seat memory, plus a seat-massage function for ultimate comfort. Heated rear seats and a third-row seat provide versatility for passengers and cargo. The cabin is refined with woodgrain interior trim and a premium sound system.

Safety and convenience are paramount with features like adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front collision warning, and a comprehensive airbag system. Additional highlights include an auto-dimming rearview mirror, hands-free power liftgate, remote engine start, and a universal garage door opener, making every journey effortless and secure.
