Experience the 2023 Ford F-150 XLT Super Crew in Agate Black Metallic, a rugged and capable 4x4 truck designed for both work and adventure with advanced safety and towing features.

Powered by a 2.7L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission, this F-150 offers selectable drive modes including tow/haul and off-road settings like deep snow/sand and mud/rut. The 4WD drivetrain and locking limited-slip differential provide confident traction on all terrains, complemented by all-terrain tires front and rear. The Class IV trailer hitch receiver with smart trailer tow connector enhances towing capabilities, supported by a 7-pin wiring harness and adaptor.

Inside, the spacious Super Crew cab seats six with dark slate cloth 40/20/40 split bench seating featuring driver and passenger adjustable lumbar support and a pass-through rear seat. The interior includes modern conveniences such as Bluetooth connectivity, a backup camera, satellite radio, WiFi hotspot, and steering wheel audio controls.

Safety is paramount with features like blind spot monitoring with trailer tow coverage, front and rear collision mitigation, lane keeping assist, automatic high beams, and multiple airbags. The integrated tailgate step with lift assist and work surface adds practical utility for loading and unloading.

This Ford F-150 XLT combines power, technology, and safety to meet the demands of Canadian drivers who require versatility and reliability.
