Experience the power and versatility of the 2022 Ford F-150 XLT Super Crew with advanced safety and towing capabilities in a sleek Oxford White finish.

This 2022 Ford F-150 XLT is built for drivers who demand strength and reliability. Powered by a robust 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with a smooth 10-speed electronic automatic transmission featuring selectable drive modes, it delivers impressive performance both on and off the road. The Super Crew cab offers spacious seating for six with a practical 40/20/40 dark slate cloth bench and pass-through rear seat, perfect for accommodating passengers and gear comfortably. The 5.5-foot short bed provides ample cargo space for work or play.

Equipped with four-wheel drive and a Class IV trailer hitch, this truck is ready for towing and tough terrain. Safety is paramount with features like Pre-Collision Assist, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keeping Assist, and a comprehensive airbag system including front, side, and rear head airbags. Additional safety aids include Brake Assist, Stability and Traction Control, and Tow Hooks for added security during demanding tasks.

Convenience and connectivity come standard with a back-up camera, Bluetooth, keyless entry, and steering wheel audio controls. The F-150 XLT also offers automatic headlights, automatic high beams, and a tire pressure monitor for enhanced driving confidence.
