Experience the 2022 Ford Escape SEL in Iconic Silver Metallic, blending advanced safety and comfort with a turbocharged 1.5L EcoBoost engine and sleek design.

This used Ford Escape SEL stands out with a panoramic vista roof that fills the cabin with natural light, enhancing every journey. Equipped with a powerful 1.5L EcoBoost turbocharged 3-cylinder engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, it delivers responsive performance while maintaining fuel efficiency. The front-wheel-drive system and brake-actuated limited-slip differential provide confident handling on various road conditions.

Inside, premium synthetic Ebony Activex-trim bucket seats offer heated front seating and power adjustments including driver lumbar support for personalized comfort. The power liftgate adds convenience for loading and unloading, while the heated steering wheel ensures warmth on chilly Canadian mornings.
