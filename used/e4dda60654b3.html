Experience the 2026 Ford Bronco Sport Big Bend in Oxford White, designed for adventure with advanced 4x4 capability and smart terrain management.

This used Bronco Sport Big Bend is powered by a turbocharged 1.5L EcoBoost 3-cylinder engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, featuring auto start-stop technology for improved fuel efficiency. Its Terrain Management System offers five G.O.A.T. modes—normal, ECO, sport, slippery, and off-road—allowing you to confidently tackle diverse driving conditions. The 4-wheel drive drivetrain ensures traction and control on rugged trails or city streets alike.

Inside, the Med Light Smoked Truffle Trim interior surrounds you with comfort and convenience. Enjoy heated front bucket seats with driver-adjustable lumbar support and a power driver seat, while the rear bench includes a pass-through for added versatility. Safety is comprehensive with features such as Blind Spot Monitor, Cross-Traffic Alert, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keeping Assist, Front and Rear Collision Mitigation, and a full suite of airbags including knee and side airbags.

Additional highlights include LED headlights, rain-sensing wipers, heated mirrors, keyless entry and start, a back-up camera, rear parking aid, and a WiFi hotspot. Aluminum wheels, 4-wheel disc brakes, and tire pressure monitoring complete this capable and well-equipped SUV.
