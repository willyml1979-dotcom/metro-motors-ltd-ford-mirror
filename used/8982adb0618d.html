Experience the versatile 2018 Ford EcoSport SE in Shadow Black, a compact 4WD SUV designed for confident driving and connected adventures.

Powered by a 2.0L TI-VCT GDI I-4 engine with auto start-stop technology and paired with a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift, this EcoSport delivers responsive performance and efficiency. The 4x4 drivetrain ensures capable handling across diverse Canadian terrains and weather conditions.

Inside, enjoy comfort with heated front bucket seats, a power-adjustable driver seat with lumbar support, and ebony cloth upholstery. The pass-through rear bench seat adds practicality for transporting gear. Ambient lighting enhances the cabin atmosphere, while the leather-wrapped steering wheel offers a refined touch.

Stay connected and entertained with the SE Convenience Package, featuring the SYNC 3 system with an 8-inch capacitive touchscreen, enhanced voice recognition, and voice-activated navigation. SiriusXM Traffic and Travel Link with a 5-year prepaid subscription keeps you informed, while AppLink and 2 smart-charging USB ports support your devices. Remote start and vehicle status monitoring add convenience, complemented by a Wi-Fi hotspot for up to 10 devices.

Safety is prioritized with Blind Spot Information System (BLIS) with Cross-Traffic Alert, brake assist, stability and traction control, multiple airbags, and automatic headlights. Aluminum 17-inch painted machined wheels with all-season tires complete this well-equipped EcoSport SE.
