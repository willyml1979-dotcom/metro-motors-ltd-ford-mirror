Experience the 2023 Ford Maverick LARIAT: a versatile, turbocharged AWD crew cab truck with advanced safety and smart connectivity for confident Canadian driving.

This 2023 Ford Maverick LARIAT in Shadow Black combines power and practicality with its 2.0L EcoBoost turbocharged 4-cylinder engine paired to an 8-speed automatic transmission and all-wheel drive, delivering responsive performance in diverse conditions. The Super Crew cab comfortably seats five with Desert Brown bucket and rear bench seating, perfect for workdays or weekend adventures.

Safety is paramount with features like Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking, Front Collision Mitigation, multiple airbags including knee and side airbags, Stability and Traction Control, Brake Assist, and Child Safety Locks. Automatic headlights and high beams enhance visibility, while the Tire Pressure Monitor and 4-wheel disc brakes ensure control and reliability.

Inside, enjoy climate control, smart device integration, Bluetooth connectivity, and steering wheel audio controls for seamless entertainment and communication. The back-up camera and keyless entry add convenience, while privacy glass and floor mats complete the package. Exterior highlights include LED headlights and a temporary spare tire for peace of mind on the road.

Built for those who demand capability and safety in a compact pickup, the Ford Maverick LARIAT is ready to handle your daily drive with confidence and style.
