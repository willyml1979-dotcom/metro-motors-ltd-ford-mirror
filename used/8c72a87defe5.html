Experience the power and precision of the 2024 Ford F-150 Raptor in striking Agate Black Metallic, engineered for those who demand capability and comfort in every drive.

This Super Crew truck features a robust 3.5L V6 EcoBoost High Output engine paired with a 10-speed electronic automatic transmission with SelectShift, delivering impressive performance and control. The 4x4 drivetrain and locking limited-slip differential ensure confident handling on all terrains, complemented by all-terrain tires front and rear. Tow hooks and a tow hitch add practicality for hauling and recovery tasks.

Inside, the black leather bucket seats offer both heated and cooled front seating with adjustable lumbar support for driver and passenger, plus heated rear seats for added comfort. Power-adjustable seats with memory settings enhance convenience. The cabin is equipped with advanced safety features including blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front collision mitigation, and a comprehensive airbag system for peace of mind.

Technology includes an aerial view display system, heads-up display, adaptive cruise control, back-up camera, and Bluetooth connectivity. Exterior highlights such as LED headlights, fog lamps, heated power folding mirrors with memory, running boards, and an integrated tailgate step provide functionality and style. This F-150 Raptor blends rugged capability with refined features for the ultimate driving experience.
