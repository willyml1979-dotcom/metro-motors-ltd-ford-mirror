Discover the 2024 Ford Bronco Outer Banks in Shadow Black—a rugged yet refined 4x4 SUV designed for adventurers who demand capability and comfort on every trail and city street.

Powered by a turbocharged 2.3L EcoBoost engine paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission, this Bronco delivers responsive performance whether navigating rocky terrain or cruising highways. The advanced 4WD system with Trail Control and Trail Turn Assist ensures confident handling in challenging conditions, while the HD modular front bumper and tow hooks are built for serious off-road recovery.

Inside, the Outer Banks trim offers heated, power-adjustable bucket seats with driver lumbar support, wrapped in premium leather-trimmed vinyl for all-day comfort. The sophisticated Aerial View Display System provides a 360-degree perspective, making tight manoeuvres effortless. Stay connected and on course with the integrated navigation system and smart device integration, all complemented by a premium sound system that brings your favourite tunes to life.

Safety is paramount with features like Blind Spot Monitor, Lane Keeping Assist, Front Collision Mitigation, and Rollover Protection Bars, giving peace of mind on every journey. The convertible soft top adds versatility, allowing you to embrace the open air or shelter from the elements with ease.
