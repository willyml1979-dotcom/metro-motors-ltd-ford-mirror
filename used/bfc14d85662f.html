Experience confident adventure with the 2022 Ford Bronco Sport Outer Banks in Carbonized Grey Metallic, combining rugged capability and refined comfort for every journey.

This 4WD SUV is powered by a turbocharged 1.5L EcoBoost 3-cylinder engine paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission featuring auto start-stop technology, delivering responsive performance and efficiency. The Bronco Sport Outer Banks offers a sophisticated Navy Pier interior with heated front bucket seats, power-adjustable driver and passenger seats, and driver lumbar support for all-day comfort. The pass-through rear bench seat maximizes versatility for passengers and cargo alike.

Safety is paramount with advanced features including Blind Spot Monitor, Cross-Traffic Alert, Front Collision Mitigation, Lane Departure Warning, Lane Keeping Assist, and multiple airbags including front, side, knee, and rear head airbags. Stability and traction control systems enhance confidence on varied terrain.

Exterior highlights include LED headlights, fog lamps, heated mirrors, aluminum wheels, and all-season tires with a temporary spare for peace of mind. Convenience is enhanced with keyless entry and start, rain-sensing wipers, auto-dimming rearview mirror, and rear parking aid. Inside, enjoy Bluetooth connectivity, satellite radio, climate control, heated steering wheel, and steering wheel audio controls. The Bronco Sport Outer Banks is ready to tackle urban streets and off-road trails with style and capability.
