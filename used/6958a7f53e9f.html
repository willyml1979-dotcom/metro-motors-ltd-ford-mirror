Experience confident driving with the 2018 Ford EcoSport SE, featuring advanced 4WD capability and a sleek Moondust Silver Metallic exterior.

This used 2018 Ford EcoSport SE combines versatility and technology for drivers seeking a compact SUV with thoughtful features. Powered by a 2.0L TI-VCT GDI I-4 engine with auto start-stop technology, it offers efficient performance paired with a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission with SelectShift for enhanced control. The 4-wheel drive system ensures traction and stability across diverse Canadian road conditions.

Inside, the Ebony Cloth interior features uniquely designed heated bucket seats, including a 6-way power-adjustable driver’s seat with manual lumbar support and a 4-way manually adjustable front passenger seat. The rear bench seat includes a pass-through for added cargo flexibility. The cabin is equipped with a large 8-inch touchscreen navigation system, Bluetooth connectivity, satellite radio, and smart device integration, keeping you connected and entertained on every journey.

Safety is prioritized with multiple airbags, stability and traction control, brake assist, and integrated turn signal mirrors. Additional convenience comes from keyless entry and start, a back-up camera, rear parking aid, fog lamps, and privacy glass. The 17-inch painted machined aluminum wheels complement the sporty design, while the generic sun/moonroof adds an airy feel to the cabin.
