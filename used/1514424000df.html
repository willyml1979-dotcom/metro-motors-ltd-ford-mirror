Experience the power and versatility of the 2025 Ford F-150 Lightning XLT, a fully electric 4WD SuperCrew truck designed for capability and comfort in every drive.

This Oxford White F-150 Lightning features a dual electric motor with a standard battery, delivering instant torque and smooth acceleration through its single-speed automatic transmission. The 4WD drivetrain ensures confident handling on all terrains, complemented by all-terrain tires on both front and rear. Practicality meets durability with a 5.5-foot spray-in bedliner and a tailgate step for easy access. The truck rides on aluminum wheels and includes an 18-inch spare wheel and tire for added peace of mind.

Inside, the Dark Slate cloth interior offers heated front bucket seats with power and adjustable lumbar support for both driver and passenger, ensuring comfort on long hauls. The SuperCrew cab accommodates five passengers with a spacious rear bench and pass-through rear seat.

Safety is paramount with advanced features like blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front collision mitigation, rear collision mitigation, cross-traffic alert, and multiple airbags including knee and side airbags. Additional conveniences include an aerial view display system, heated mirrors, auto-dimming rearview mirror, and keyless start and entry.

Tow hooks and a tow hitch expand your hauling capabilities, making this electric F-150 Lightning ready for work or weekend adventures.
