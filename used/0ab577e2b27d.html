Experience confident driving in the 2022 Ford EcoSport SES 4WD with a powerful 2.0L TI-VCT GDI I-4 engine and advanced safety features for Canadian roads.

This used Ford EcoSport SES combines a robust 2.0L four-cylinder engine with automatic start-stop technology and a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift for responsive control. Its four-wheel drive system ensures enhanced traction and stability in varying weather conditions, ideal for navigating urban streets or rugged paths. The exterior shines in Moondust Silver Metallic, complemented by aluminum wheels and performance tires front and rear, while a rear spoiler adds a sporty touch.

Inside, the cabin offers seating for five with heated front bucket seats, including a power-adjustable driver seat with lumbar support for comfort on longer drives. The leather-wrapped steering wheel includes audio controls and heating, perfect for chilly mornings. A power moonroof invites natural light, enhancing the spacious feel.

Safety is paramount with features such as blind spot monitoring, cross-traffic alert, multiple airbags including knee and side airbags, stability and traction control, and an integrated back-up camera with rear parking aid. Additional conveniences include keyless entry and start, heated mirrors, fog lamps, rain-sensing wipers, and an auto-dimming rearview mirror for a safer, more comfortable driving experience.
