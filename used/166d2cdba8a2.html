Experience the power and luxury of the 2022 Ford F-150 Platinum Super Crew, featuring advanced off-road capabilities and premium comfort for every journey.

This Agate Black Metallic 2022 Ford F-150 Platinum is equipped with a robust 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission, offering selectable drive modes including sport, tow/haul, and off-road settings like rock crawl and hill descent control. The FX4 Off-Road Package enhances durability with off-road tuned front shock absorbers, monotube rear shocks, skid plates, and an electronic locking differential with a 3.31 axle ratio, perfect for rugged terrain. The Max Trailer Tow Package boosts towing capacity and adds a heavy-duty electric parking brake with auto-hold for secure hauling.

Inside, the crew cab boasts black leather seats with cooling, heating, and massage functions, plus power-adjustable driver and passenger seats with lumbar support and memory settings. The panoramic roof and power retractable running boards add convenience and style. Safety is comprehensive, featuring blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, adaptive cruise control, and a suite of airbags. Additional highlights include an aerial view display system, automatic parking, heated steering wheel, LED headlights, and a premium sound system, making this F-150 a versatile and capable truck for work and play.
