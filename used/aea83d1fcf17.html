Experience the thrill of driving a 2023 Ford Mustang GT Premium Fastback with a powerful 5.0L Ti-VCT V8 engine and precision 6-speed manual transmission.

This Shadow Black Mustang GT Premium is engineered for enthusiasts who crave control and performance. The 5.0L Ti-VCT V8 with Port Fueled Direct Injection delivers robust power, paired with a 6-speed manual transmission featuring a dual mass flywheel, twin disc clutch, and rev matching for smooth, responsive shifts. Rear-wheel drive combined with a locking limited-slip differential and performance tires front and rear ensures exceptional handling on every curve.

Inside, the Ebony leather-trimmed cabin offers power-adjustable bucket seats and a pass-through rear bench, blending comfort with sporty style. The GT Performance Package enhances driving dynamics, while the active valve performance exhaust provides an exhilarating soundtrack. A 12-speaker B&O sound system fills the cabin with premium audio quality.

Safety is comprehensive with blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front collision mitigation, and automatic high beams, complemented by multiple airbags and stability control. Additional features like LED headlights, rain-sensing wipers, back-up camera, and keyless start add convenience and confidence.

This Mustang GT Premium is a true sports car designed for those who appreciate raw power, precision handling, and advanced technology in a striking fastback silhouette.
