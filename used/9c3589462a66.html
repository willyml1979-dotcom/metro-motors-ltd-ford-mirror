Experience the thrill of advanced electric performance with the 2022 Ford Mustang Mach-E GT Performance Edition in striking Grabber Blue Metallic, designed for the discerning driver.

This AWD electric sports utility vehicle features a powerful RR primary and upgraded secondary electric motor, delivering enhanced performance through a smooth single-speed automatic transmission. Its active suspension and performance tires front and rear ensure precise handling and a dynamic driving experience. The Mustang Mach-E GT Performance Edition offers premium synthetic heated bucket seats with unique Miko perforated reflective inserts, 8-way power adjustments, lumbar support, and memory settings for both driver and passenger, wrapped in Prfm Grey Activex material for comfort and style.

Safety is comprehensive, including blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front collision mitigation, rear collision mitigation, automatic headlights with high beams, and a suite of airbags including knee and side airbags. Convenience features abound with adaptive cruise control, keyless entry and start, power folding mirrors with heating, a power liftgate, and a back-up camera complemented by rear parking aid and cross-traffic alert.

Inside, enjoy a premium sound system, multi-zone climate control, heated steering wheel, and smart device integration with Bluetooth connectivity. The interior protection package adds durable front and rear floor liners plus a cargo area protector, perfect for active lifestyles. This Mustang Mach-E blends cutting-edge electric power with refined comfort and advanced technology.
