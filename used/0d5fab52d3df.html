Experience the 2024 Ford Maverick LARIAT Hybrid in striking Hot Pepper Red Metallic with advanced safety and luxury features designed for modern Canadian drivers.

This 2WD small pickup truck combines efficiency and capability with its 2.5L hybrid engine paired to an automatic continuous variable transmission (CVT) and front-wheel drive, offering smooth performance and eco-friendly driving. The Super Crew cab comfortably seats five with premium synthetic bucket seats, including a heated and power-adjustable driver’s seat with lumbar support for added comfort on long drives. The interior features Desert Brown trim that complements the sophisticated LARIAT Luxury Package.

Safety is paramount with Ford Co-Pilot 360 Assist, featuring adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, lane departure warning, front collision mitigation, and evasive assist. Additional safety elements include automatic headlights and high beams, brake assist, multiple airbags, stability and traction control, and child safety locks.

The Maverick’s exterior is rugged yet refined, equipped with aluminum wheels, all-season tires, a bed liner for cargo protection, heated mirrors, LED headlights, and a tow hitch ready for your weekend adventures. Inside, enjoy conveniences like a heated steering wheel, back-up camera, Bluetooth connectivity, premium sound system, and multi-zone climate control, making every journey comfortable and connected.
