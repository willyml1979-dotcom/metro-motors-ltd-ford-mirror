Experience the 2022 Ford F-150 Lariat in Carbonized Grey Metallic, combining rugged capability with refined style and advanced technology for demanding Canadian drivers.

This Super Crew Crew Cab Pickup features a powerful 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with a smooth 10-speed electronic automatic transmission, offering selectable drive modes including sport, tow/haul, and deep snow/sand for versatile performance in all conditions. The 4WD system with locking limited-slip differential ensures confident handling on challenging terrain.

The Lariat Sport Appearance Package adds distinctive touches such as 18-inch 6-spoke machined-aluminum wheels with gloss black pockets, body-colour door handles and bumpers, a dark grille with black accents, and an angular step bar in accent colour. Inside, enjoy a unique interior finish with vintage carmine IP topper, door top roll, and console armrest, complemented by black leather-trimmed bucket seats.

Advanced safety features include front collision mitigation, lane departure warning and lane keeping assist, driver monitoring, and a 360-degree camera system for enhanced awareness. Pro Power Onboard provides convenient on-the-go power supply, while the twin panel moonroof invites natural light into the spacious cabin. Additional technology includes Bluetooth connectivity, WiFi hotspot, smart device integration, and steering wheel audio controls for seamless connectivity. The 6.5-foot short bed offers practical cargo space with a conventional spare tire for peace of mind.
