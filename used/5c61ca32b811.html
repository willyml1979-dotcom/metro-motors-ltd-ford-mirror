Experience the 2025 Ford F-150 XLT with 3.5L EcoBoost, Black Appearance and Tow/Haul Packages, designed for power, capability, and advanced safety on every drive.

This Super Crew pickup features a robust 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission with SelectShift, delivering smooth performance and responsive power. The 4x4 drivetrain and all-terrain tires on both front and rear ensure confident handling across diverse road conditions. The 5.5-foot short bed is protected by a durable spray liner, ready for heavy-duty hauling.

Inside, the black sport cloth 40/console/40 seating comfortably accommodates six, with driver adjustable lumbar support and a pass-through rear seat for added versatility. Safety is front and centre with features including Blind Spot Monitor, Cross-Traffic Alert, Lane Keeping Assist, Front and Rear Collision Mitigation, and Automatic Highbeams. Tow hooks and a tow hitch enhance your towing capabilities, while LED headlights and fog lamps improve visibility in all conditions.

Convenience features like a back-up camera, rear parking aid, heated mirrors, keyless entry, Bluetooth connection, and WiFi hotspot keep you connected and in control. The Ford F-150 XLT is built to handle demanding tasks while providing comfort and advanced technology for your everyday driving needs.
