2019 Ford Transit Van in White Gold Metallic – Reliable Cargo Van with Advanced Safety and Convenient Features for Efficient Workdays

This 2019 Ford Transit Van is a full-size cargo van designed for those who need dependable performance and practical functionality. Powered by a robust V6 cylinder engine paired with a 6-speed automatic transmission featuring dual shift mode, this rear-wheel-drive van offers smooth handling and flexibility on the road. Its flex fuel capability adds versatility for fuel options, helping to manage operating costs.

Safety is a priority with multiple airbags including driver, passenger, front side, and front head airbags, along with brake assist, stability control, traction control, and ABS with 4-wheel disc brakes. Tow hooks provide added utility for hauling or recovery needs. The tire pressure monitoring system helps maintain optimal tire performance and safety.

The van’s exterior is finished in a durable White Gold Metallic with three doors including a third passenger door for easier access. It rides on all-season tires front and rear, complemented by a conventional spare tire for peace of mind.

Inside, the cabin features bucket seats for two and an adjustable steering wheel for driver comfort. Convenience is enhanced with keyless entry, power door locks, power windows, and a back-up camera to assist with manoeuvring in tight spaces. Auxiliary audio input adds entertainment options during work trips.

This Ford Transit Van is a solid choice for professionals requiring a reliable, well-equipped cargo vehicle.
