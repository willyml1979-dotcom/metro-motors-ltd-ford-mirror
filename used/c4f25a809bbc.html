Experience confident driving in the 2025 Ford Escape Active, featuring advanced safety technologies and a turbocharged 1.5L EcoBoost engine with efficient auto start-stop.

This Ford Escape Active in striking Vapor Blue Metallic pairs a turbocharged 3-cylinder 1.5L EcoBoost engine with an 8-speed automatic transmission and front-wheel drive, delivering responsive performance and fuel efficiency. The Cold Weather Package ensures comfort during chilly Canadian seasons, while the Remote Engine Start lets you warm up your vehicle before stepping inside.

Safety is paramount with features such as Blind Spot Monitor, Cross-Traffic Alert, Front Collision Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist, and Rear Collision Mitigation. The suite also includes Automatic Headlights with Highbeams, Brake Assist, and multiple airbags including knee and side airbags for comprehensive protection. Driver Monitoring and Lane Departure Warning add extra layers of awareness on every trip.

Inside, the Dark Space Gray Uni Cloth seats provide comfort for five, with bucket seats up front and a pass-through rear bench. Convenience is enhanced by keyless entry and start, Bluetooth connectivity, a back-up camera, and a multi-zone climate control system. Aluminum wheels, privacy glass, and a rear spoiler complete the sporty and practical exterior look of this 2WD SUV.
