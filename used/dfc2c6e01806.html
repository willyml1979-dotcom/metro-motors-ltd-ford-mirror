Experience confident driving with the 2021 Ford Edge Titanium, featuring advanced safety, premium comfort, and a powerful Twin-Scroll 2.0L EcoBoost engine paired with all-wheel drive.

This Ford Edge Titanium is designed for those who value both performance and refinement. Its turbocharged 2.0L EcoBoost engine delivers responsive power, complemented by an 8-speed automatic transmission with dual shift mode for smooth, efficient driving. The all-wheel-drive system with a brake-actuated limited-slip differential ensures excellent traction in diverse Canadian conditions. The Canadian Touring Package adds 20-inch machined aluminum wheels and a panoramic roof, inviting natural light and enhancing the cabin’s spacious feel. Inside, ebony leather bucket seats with heated front seats and power-adjustable driver and passenger seats featuring lumbar support and seat memory provide tailored comfort. The Cold Weather Package includes a heated steering wheel and front and rear floor liners with carpet mats, perfect for chilly mornings. The Cargo Accessory Package offers a cargo mat, retractable cargo area cover, and rear bumper protector to keep your gear secure and your vehicle protected. Safety is comprehensive with features like blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front collision mitigation, automatic high beams, and a backup camera. Additional amenities include a hands-free power liftgate, remote engine start, and premium sound system, making every journey enjoyable and convenient.
