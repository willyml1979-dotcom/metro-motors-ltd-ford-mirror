Experience the power and versatility of the 2024 Ford F-150 Lightning LARIAT in striking Agate Black Metallic, designed for drivers who demand electric performance and advanced technology.

This all-electric Super Crew pickup features a Dual Motor setup paired with an Extended Range 131 kWh lithium-ion battery, delivering impressive AWD capability and a single-speed automatic transmission for smooth, responsive driving. The 5.5-foot short bed is complemented by an integrated tailgate step and running boards, making loading and unloading effortless. Aluminum wheels and a conventional spare tire enhance durability and convenience.

Inside, the Black Activex Cloth cabin offers heated and cooled bucket seats with power adjustments and memory settings for both driver and passenger, including adjustable lumbar support. Rear passengers enjoy heated seats and a pass-through rear bench, ensuring comfort for all five occupants. The panoramic sun/moonroof floods the interior with natural light, while the leather-wrapped steering wheel features heating for added comfort.

Safety is comprehensive with adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, and a suite of airbags including knee and side airbags. Additional tech includes an aerial view display system, auto-dimming rearview mirror, heated mirrors, and keyless entry with push-button start. Fast charging capability and a tow hitch round out this capable electric truck, ready for work or weekend adventures.
