Experience the 2024 Ford F-150 STX in Agate Black Metallic, a rugged 4x4 crew cab pickup with advanced safety and powerful performance for Canadian drivers.

This 2024 Ford F-150 STX combines capability and comfort with its 2.7L V6 EcoBoost engine paired to an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission featuring SelectShift for responsive control. The 4x4 drivetrain and all-terrain tires front and rear ensure confident handling on diverse road conditions. Its 5.5-foot short bed offers practical cargo space, while tow hooks and a tow hitch enhance its utility for hauling and towing tasks.

Inside, the Super Crew cabin seats five with durable charcoal black cloth bucket and rear bench seats, both equipped with adjustable lumbar support for driver and passenger comfort. The interior is designed for convenience with features like keyless entry, power door locks, power mirrors, and an adjustable steering wheel. Technology includes Bluetooth connectivity, a back-up camera, satellite radio, and a WiFi hotspot to keep you connected on the go.

Safety is paramount with automatic headlights, high beams, blind spot monitoring, cross-traffic alert, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, and multiple airbags including front, side, and rear collision mitigation systems. Aluminum wheels, fog lamps, heated mirrors, and privacy glass complete this well-rounded truck built for work and everyday driving.
