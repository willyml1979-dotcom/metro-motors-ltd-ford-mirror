Experience the 2026 Ford F-150 XLT in Carbonized Grey Metallic, a rugged 4x4 SuperCrew truck built for power, comfort, and advanced safety on every journey.

Powered by a turbocharged V6 engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission featuring dual shift mode, this F-150 delivers impressive performance and control whether on-road or off. The 4WD drivetrain and all-terrain tires front and rear ensure confident handling across diverse terrains. Inside, the spacious SuperCrew cab comfortably seats six with black sport cloth bucket and rear bench seats, including heated front seats and a power-adjustable driver seat with lumbar support for long-haul comfort. The 5.5-foot box with integrated tailgate step enhances loading ease for work or recreation.

Safety is a priority with features like blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, automatic headlights with high beams, and a comprehensive airbag system. Convenience is boosted by keyless entry and start, remote engine start, an aerial view display system, backup camera, rear parking aid, and an auto-dimming rearview mirror. Additional practical touches include heated mirrors, fog lamps, tow hooks, and a tow hitch for your hauling needs. The Ford F-150 XLT blends capability and technology for drivers who demand reliability and versatility.
