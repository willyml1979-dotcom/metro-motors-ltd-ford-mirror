Experience the 2026 Ford Ranger XLT in Avalanche – a rugged, tech-savvy 4x4 crew cab built for Canadian adventurers and hardworking drivers who demand capability and comfort.

Powered by a turbocharged 2.3L EcoBoost engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission, this Ranger delivers responsive power and efficiency on every terrain. The FX4 Off-Road Package enhances your drive with selectable modes for mud, ruts, sand, and more, plus an electronic-locking rear differential and off-road tuned shocks to conquer challenging trails. The Advanced Towing Package, including a Class IV hitch, trailer brake controller, and Pro Trailer Backup Assist, makes hauling your gear or trailer effortless and safe.

Inside, heated cloth bucket seats with power and lumbar adjustments provide all-day comfort, while the Convenience Package offers Pro Power Onboard 400W for tools or devices, LED bed lighting, and a wireless charging pad to keep you connected. Safety is paramount with blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, and a comprehensive suite of airbags.

Finished in striking Avalanche with black accents from the Black Appearance Package, this Ranger combines aggressive style with practical features like all-terrain tires and a 5-foot short bed, perfect for urban errands or weekend escapades. The 2026 Ford Ranger XLT is ready for your next journey.
