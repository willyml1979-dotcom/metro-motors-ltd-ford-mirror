Built for demanding work and rugged terrain, the 2026 Ford Super Duty F-350 SRW XLT Crew Cab combines power, durability, and advanced technology to meet the needs of serious drivers.

Powered by a robust 6.7L Power Stroke V8 Turbo Diesel engine, this truck delivers exceptional torque and towing capability, ideal for hauling heavy loads or towing trailers with confidence. The TorqShift 10-speed automatic transmission offers smooth shifting and selectable drive modes including tow/haul and off-road, ensuring optimal performance in any condition. With its 4x4 drivetrain and locking limited-slip differential, the F-350 SRW conquers challenging terrain and slippery roads effortlessly.

Designed for utility and convenience, the truck features six overhead upfitter switches, allowing seamless integration of aftermarket equipment. The integrated tailgate step and running boards provide easy access to the 6.75-foot box, making loading and unloading more efficient. Safety is paramount with front collision mitigation, automatic high beams, and a comprehensive airbag system.

Inside, the medium dark slate cloth 40/20/40 split bench seat offers comfort and versatility, including an 8-way power driver seat with lumbar support and a centre console with locking storage. Heated mirrors, a back-up camera, and Bluetooth connectivity round out the package, making the F-350 SRW a reliable partner for both work and everyday driving.
