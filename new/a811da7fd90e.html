Discover the 2027 Ford Transit Cargo Van in Oxford White, designed for efficient cargo transport with advanced safety and smart technology features.

Powered by a robust 3.5L PFDI V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission featuring overdrive and SelectShift, this rear-wheel-drive van delivers reliable performance and smooth shifting. The Transit Cargo Van is equipped with steel wheels and all-season tires front and rear, complemented by a conventional spare tire with a 3-ton jack and tool kit, ensuring readiness for any road situation.

Inside, the dark Palazzo Grey vinyl interior offers durable bucket seats and vinyl flooring with wheel well liners, built to withstand heavy use. The full rear compartment lighting includes LED lights at the C-pillar and D-pillar, along with a rear compartment LED switch, providing excellent visibility when loading or unloading cargo.

Safety is paramount with features such as automatic headlights, automatic high beams, front collision mitigation, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, and a comprehensive airbag system including driver, passenger, front side, and front head airbags. Stability and traction control, brake assist, and tow hooks add further confidence on the road.

Additional conveniences include keyless entry and start with four key fobs, Bluetooth connection, back-up camera, rear parking aid, cruise control, adjustable steering wheel, rain-sensing wipers, and a WiFi hotspot, making this van a smart choice for business owners and drivers who demand practicality and safety.
