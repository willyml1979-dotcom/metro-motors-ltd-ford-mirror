Experience the power and versatility of the 2026 Ford F-150 XLT SuperCrew 4x4 with a commanding 5.0L V8 engine and advanced safety features.

This Agate Black Metallic Ford F-150 XLT is built for drivers who demand strength and capability. Powered by a robust 5.0L V8 engine paired with a smooth electronic 10-speed automatic transmission, it delivers confident performance on any terrain. The 4x4 drivetrain and locking limited-slip differential enhance traction and control, while all-terrain tires and tow hooks prepare you for tough jobs or weekend adventures.

Inside, the spacious SuperCrew cab comfortably seats six with black sport cloth bucket and rear bench seats. Enjoy heated front seats, a power driver seat with adjustable lumbar support, and a pass-through rear seat for added convenience. The cabin features multi-zone climate control, an adjustable steering wheel, and smart device integration with Bluetooth connectivity and a WiFi hotspot to keep you connected on the go.

Safety is paramount with automatic headlights and high beams, blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, cross-traffic alert, and a comprehensive airbag system. Additional driver aids include an aerial view display, back-up camera, rear parking aid, and remote engine start for ease and confidence every time you drive.
