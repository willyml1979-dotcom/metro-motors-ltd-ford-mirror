The 2026 Ford E-Series Cutaway is engineered for those who demand more from their vehicles. The rear-wheel drive enhances traction and control, ensuring a smooth ride even when fully loaded. The 6-speed automatic transmission with Tow Haul mode provides seamless gear shifts and superior towing capabilities, perfect for those who need to transport heavy loads with confidence.

The Oxford White exterior exudes professionalism, while the Dark Gray Cloth Captain’s Chair interior offers comfort during long hauls.
