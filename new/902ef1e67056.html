Experience commanding capability and refined comfort with the 2027 Ford Explorer Tremor in striking Carbonized Grey Metallic, designed for drivers who demand performance and luxury in every journey.

Powered by a robust 3.0L EcoBoost V6 engine with auto start-stop technology and paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission, this 4x4 SUV delivers confident handling on all terrains. The Tremor’s advanced drivetrain features a locking limited-slip differential and all-terrain tires front and rear, ensuring traction and stability whether on highways or rugged trails.

Inside, premium synthetic Onyx Miko suede sport captain’s chairs offer heated, ventilated, and power-adjustable seating with lumbar support and massage functions, providing six-passenger comfort with a third-row power-fold seat that folds flat for versatile cargo space. The panoramic opening Vista roof floods the cabin with natural light, while ambient lighting enhances the refined interior atmosphere.

Technology is elevated with the B&O Sound System by Bang & Olufsen, featuring 14 speakers and a subwoofer for immersive audio. Safety is comprehensive, including Blind Spot Monitor, Front Collision Mitigation, Lane Keeping Assist, and BlueCruise hands-free highway driving with a 90-day trial. Additional conveniences include a hands-free power liftgate, power-folding heated mirrors with auto-dimming and memory, and SiriusXM with a 3-year subscription for endless entertainment on the road.
