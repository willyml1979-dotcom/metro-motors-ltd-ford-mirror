Experience the 2027 Ford Explorer Platinum in striking Vapor Blue Metallic, designed for drivers who demand luxury, advanced technology, and rugged capability in a sophisticated SUV.

This new Ford Explorer Platinum is powered by a turbocharged 2.3L EcoBoost I-4 engine paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission, delivering confident performance with 4x4 capability. The exterior features a panoramic sun/moonroof and LED headlights, complemented by aluminum wheels and performance tires front and rear, perfect for Canadian roads and adventures. Safety is paramount with an extensive suite including Blind Spot Monitor, Lane Keeping Assist, Front and Rear Collision Mitigation, Cross-Traffic Alert, and Driver Monitoring systems, ensuring peace of mind on every journey.

Inside, the Mojave Dusk premium leather seats with quilting and stitching provide exceptional comfort and style. The front sport captain chairs are heated, ventilated, and feature multicontour massaging with Active Motion technology, plus 8-way power adjustment with memory for the driver and 6-way power for the passenger. Additional luxury touches include a heated leather steering wheel, woodgrain interior trim, and a hands-free power liftgate for convenience. The advanced Aerial View Display System and adaptive cruise control enhance driving ease, while the premium sound system and WiFi hotspot keep you connected and entertained. This Explorer is equipped to handle both daily commutes and weekend getaways with unmatched refinement and capability.
