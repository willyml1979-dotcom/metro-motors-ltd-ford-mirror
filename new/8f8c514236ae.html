Experience the 2027 Ford Super Duty F-350 SRW Platinum in Star White Metallic Tri-Coat, engineered for exceptional power and advanced off-road capability.

Powered by a robust 6.7L HO Power Stroke V8 Turbo Diesel engine paired with a TorqShift 10-speed automatic transmission, this 4x4 crew cab pickup delivers impressive torque and versatility. The Tremor Off-Road Package enhances rugged performance with unique front springs for increased ride height, front limited slip differential, performance shock absorbers, rock crawl mode, trail control, and skid plates for transfer case and fuel tank protection. Equipped with LT285/75R18E BSW all-terrain tires on 18” ebony black machined aluminum wheels, this truck is ready for challenging terrain.

Inside, the Black Onyx and Platinum Blue interior features unique platinum leather 40/console/40 seats with multi-contour heated and ventilated front seats, 8-way power adjustments including lumbar support, seat memory, and a seat-massage function for ultimate comfort. The twin-panel power moonroof and panoramic roof provide an expansive view, while the Pro Power Onboard system offers 2kW of power from dual alternators for worksite convenience.

Safety is comprehensive with adaptive cruise control, blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, and a suite of airbags. Additional features include a 360-degree aerial view camera, SiriusXM with a 3-year subscription, keyless entry with SecuriCode keypad, and six overhead upfitter switches for custom equipment integration. This Super Duty F-350 Platinum combines rugged capability with premium comfort and advanced technology for demanding drivers.
