Built for demanding work, the 2027 Ford Super Duty F-650 Straight Frame in Oxford White delivers exceptional power and durability with its 6.7L Power Stroke V8 turbo diesel engine producing 330 HP and an impressive 850 lb.-ft. of torque. Designed as a medium-duty truck with a regular cab chassis-cab body style, it features a robust 10-speed automatic transmission with dual shift mode and rear-wheel drive paired with dual rear wheels for superior traction and load capacity.

This truck is equipped with a 19,000 lb. single-speed Dana S19-140 rear axle featuring quiet gears and heavy-duty 190 wheel ends, complemented by a single channel straight “C” frame made of heat-treated alloy steel for maximum strength. The suspension includes 12,000 lb. capacity parabolic taper-leaf springs with dual double-acting shock absorbers for enhanced stability under load.

The exterior appearance group adds a chrome grille surround, full-width chrome front bumper, and chromed fender badges, while heated and motorized dual rectangular mirrors with chrome caps improve visibility. Practical running boards and tow hooks enhance functionality. Inside, a high-level 8-inch enhanced cluster display provides vital vehicle information, and the power equipment group includes power windows, door locks, and remote keyless entry for convenience. A rear view camera with mirror display is ready for upfitter installation, making this truck ready for tough jobs with advanced features tailored to professional drivers.
