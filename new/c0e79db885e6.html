Experience exceptional capability and safety in the 2027 Ford Transit Cargo Van, designed for professionals who demand reliability and advanced features in a full-size work van.

Powered by a robust 3.5L PFDI V6 engine paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission featuring overdrive and SelectShift, this rear-wheel-drive van delivers dependable performance tailored for heavy-duty tasks. The locking limited-slip differential enhances traction, ensuring control in diverse driving conditions. Its durable steel wheels and all-season tires provide stability and resilience on the road.

Inside, the spacious cabin accommodates two with dark Palazzo grey cloth bucket seats, including a 2-way manual driver seat with adjustable lumbar support and armrests for added comfort during long hauls. Practicality is enhanced by vinyl floor coverings with wheel well liners, simplifying cargo cleanup.

Safety is paramount with features such as front collision mitigation, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, and driver monitoring. Automatic headlights, high beams, and rain-sensing wipers adapt to changing conditions, while a comprehensive airbag system and stability and traction control offer peace of mind.

Additional conveniences include keyless entry and start, Bluetooth connectivity, a back-up camera, rear parking aid, cruise control, and WiFi hotspot capability. The package is completed with a conventional spare tire, 3-ton jack, tool kit, and two extra key fobs, making the 2027 Ford Transit Cargo Van a smart choice for your business needs.
