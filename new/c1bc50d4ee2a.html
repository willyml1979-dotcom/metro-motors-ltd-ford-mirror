Built to handle demanding jobs, the 2027 Ford Super Duty F-750 Straight Frame is a robust medium-duty truck designed for serious performance and durability. Powered by a 6.7L Power Stroke V8 turbo diesel engine delivering 330 horsepower at 2600 RPM and an impressive 850 lb.-ft. of torque at 1500 RPM, this truck offers exceptional towing and hauling capabilities. The 10-speed automatic transmission with dual shift mode ensures smooth and efficient power delivery, paired with rear-wheel drive and dual rear wheels for enhanced stability under heavy loads.

The chassis features a reinforced outer "C" channel with heat-treated alloy steel, providing outstanding strength and rigidity. Suspension includes 12,000 lb. capacity parabolic taper-leaf springs in front and multi-leaf springs rated up to 23,000 lb. at the rear, supported by dual double-acting shock absorbers for improved ride control. Trailer tow powered mirrors with power telescoping, folding, heating, and integrated turn signals improve visibility and safety on the road.

Inside, the steel gray cloth seats offer durability, while the enhanced 8-inch digital cluster provides vital vehicle information including oil pressure, coolant temperature, DEF levels, and transmission temperature. Additional features like power windows, keyless entry, cruise control, and a back-up camera with mirror display support ease of operation. The truck’s rugged design is complemented by running boards and tow hooks, making it ready for any tough job.
