Experience the thrill of cutting-edge electric performance combined with iconic Mustang style in the 2026 Ford Mustang Mach-E Select, finished in striking Adriatic Blue Metallic.

Designed for drivers who crave dynamic handling and advanced technology, the Mach-E Select features a dual motor all-wheel-drive system that delivers instant torque and confident traction in all conditions. Its 73 kWh usable capacity battery supports fast charging, making longer journeys effortless and convenient. Step inside to find premium synthetic Black Onyx Activex bucket seats with 8-way power adjustment and lumbar support, ensuring comfort on every drive. The spacious interior is thoughtfully crafted, with a pass-through rear seat for added versatility.

Safety is paramount with a comprehensive suite including Blind Spot Monitor, Lane Keeping Assist, Front and Rear Collision Mitigation, and Driver Monitoring, providing peace of mind on busy roads. The adaptive cruise control and rain-sensing wipers add convenience and ease during variable weather and traffic. Aluminum wheels paired with performance tires front and rear enhance grip and handling, while the rear spoiler adds a sporty touch to the sleek SUV silhouette.

For the discerning driver who values innovation, style, and performance, the Mustang Mach-E Select offers an electrifying driving experience that stands apart on Canadian roads.
