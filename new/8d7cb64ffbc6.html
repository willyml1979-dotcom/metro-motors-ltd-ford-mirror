Experience the 2026 Ford F-150 LARIAT in Avalanche – a rugged yet refined 4x4 crew cab designed for Canadian drivers who demand power, versatility, and advanced technology on every journey.

Built to handle tough terrain and heavy-duty tasks, this F-150 features a turbocharged 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with a smooth electronic 10-speed automatic transmission. Select from multiple drive modes including tow/haul and deep snow/sand, ensuring optimal performance whether you’re hauling gear or navigating challenging road conditions. The integrated trailer brake controller and power telescoping trailer tow mirrors with LED spotlights make towing safer and more convenient.

Inside, the cabin offers premium synthetic seats trimmed in Black Activex with heated and ventilated front seats, power lumbar adjustments, and memory settings for driver comfort on long hauls. The Pro Access Tailgate with work surface, LED box lighting, and bed storage boxes enhance utility, while 6-inch extended running boards provide easy access. Safety is paramount with blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, and front collision mitigation, giving you confidence on every drive.
