Experience the 2026 Ford F-150 LARIAT: a rugged yet refined Super Crew truck built for Canadian drivers who demand power, versatility, and advanced off-road capability.

Powered by a robust 5.0L V8 engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission, this F-150 delivers confident performance whether towing heavy loads or navigating challenging terrain. The FX4 Off-Road Package enhances trail readiness with rock crawl mode, skid plates protecting vital components, hill descent control, and locking rear differential, perfect for weekend adventures or worksite demands. The Tow/Haul Package with integrated trailer brake controller ensures safe, controlled towing every time.

Inside, the Black Activex-trimmed cabin boasts heated and ventilated 10-way power driver seats with lumbar support and memory functions, ensuring comfort on long hauls. The panoramic roof invites natural light, while advanced tech like the aerial view display system, adaptive cruise control, and heads-up display keep you connected and aware. Power telescoping, heated trailer tow mirrors with LED spotlights provide excellent visibility for manoeuvring trailers or accessing tight spots.

With rugged all-terrain tires, running boards, and a 6.5-foot short bed, this F-150 LARIAT blends capability with style, ready to tackle Canadian roads and backcountry with confidence.
