Experience the thrill of cutting-edge electric performance with the 2026 Ford Mustang Mach-E GT, designed for drivers who demand power, precision, and advanced technology in every journey.

This all-wheel-drive electric SUV combines a potent dual motor setup with a 91kWh extended range battery, delivering exhilarating acceleration and confident handling on Canadian roads. The panoramic fixed-glass roof with infrared reflective technology floods the cabin with natural light while reducing heat, creating an inviting atmosphere for every drive. Inside, the heated perforated Activex performance seats offer superior comfort and support, featuring 10-way power adjustments with memory for the driver and 8-way for the passenger, ensuring a tailored fit on long trips or spirited drives.

Stay connected and in control with the aerial view display system and smart device integration, while adaptive cruise control and advanced safety features like blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, and front collision mitigation provide peace of mind. Aluminum wheels wrapped in front and rear performance tires enhance grip and style, complemented by a sleek rear spoiler that underscores this Mustang’s sporty character. For those who appreciate innovation and dynamic driving, the Mustang Mach-E GT is an electrifying choice that blends iconic design with modern sophistication.
