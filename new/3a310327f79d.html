Built for Canadian drivers who demand toughness and versatility, the 2026 Ford F-150 XL Super Crew 4x4 combines rugged capability with advanced technology and safety features designed for every terrain and task.

Powered by a robust 3.5L V6 EcoBoost engine paired with an electronic 10-speed automatic transmission, this F-150 delivers impressive towing capacity and confident handling in all weather conditions. The selectable drive modes—including tow/haul, slippery, and deep snow/sand—ensure optimal performance whether hauling heavy loads or navigating challenging roads. Its 4x4 drivetrain, locking limited-slip differential, and all-terrain tires provide exceptional traction and stability on rugged trails or snowy highways.

Safety is paramount with blind spot monitoring, lane keeping assist, front and rear collision mitigation, and a comprehensive airbag system, giving peace of mind on every journey. The integrated tailgate step and tow hooks add practicality for work and outdoor adventures, while heated mirrors and LED fog lamps enhance visibility in harsh Canadian climates.

Inside, durable medium dark slate vinyl seats and a spacious crew cab accommodate both crew and cargo comfortably. With a rear window defroster and privacy glass, this truck is ready for early winter mornings and long hauls. The F-150 XL is built to handle your toughest jobs without compromise.
