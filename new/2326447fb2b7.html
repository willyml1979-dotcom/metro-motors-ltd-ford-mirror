Powerful 2027 Ford Super Duty F-650 Straight Frame Medium-Duty Truck with Turbocharged Diesel Engine and Advanced Trailer Tow Features

The 2027 Ford Super Duty F-650 Straight Frame is engineered for demanding work with a robust 6.7L Power Stroke V8 turbo diesel engine delivering 330 horsepower at 2600 RPM and an impressive 850 lb.-ft. of torque at 1500 RPM. This medium-duty truck features a Ford Torqshift HD 10-speed automatic transmission with dual shift mode and PTO provision, paired with rear-wheel drive and dual rear wheels for superior traction and load capacity.

Designed for heavy-duty applications, it boasts a straight ‘C’ 14.18-inch steel frame rated at 1,701,600 RBM, supported by 10,000 lb. capacity parabolic taper-leaf springs and dual double-acting shock absorbers for durability and stability. The truck’s rear wheels are 19.5" white powder-coated steel rims with an 8-hole design, ensuring strength and reliability.

Driver convenience and safety are enhanced with power folding, heated trailer tow mirrors featuring integrated turn signals and clearance lights, along with a 7-way trailer connection socket wired for separate stop, tail, and turn signals, including electric trailer brake accommodation. Additional features include running boards, automatic headlights, traction control, tow hooks, and a durable vinyl interior with steel gray cloth seating. This Ford F-650 is ready to handle your toughest jobs with confidence and capability.
