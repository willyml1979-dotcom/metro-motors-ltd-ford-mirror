2027 Ford Transit Cargo Van in Oxford White with advanced safety, versatile cargo lighting, and efficient 3.5L V6 engine for reliable commercial performance.

The new 2027 Ford Transit Cargo Van is designed to meet the demands of professionals who require a dependable and practical work vehicle. Powered by a robust 3.5L PFDI V6 engine paired with a 10-speed automatic transmission featuring overdrive and SelectShift, this rear-wheel-drive van delivers smooth and efficient performance. Its locking limited-slip differential enhances traction, making it suitable for various driving conditions.

Inside, the van offers two ebony cloth bucket seats with manual adjustments and driver lumbar support, ensuring comfort during long workdays. The interior is equipped with full rear compartment LED lighting at the C-pillar, D-pillar, and an accessible rear switch, providing excellent visibility for loading and unloading cargo. The front and rear vinyl floor covering with wheel well liners adds durability for heavy-duty use.

Safety is a priority with features such as automatic headlights, high beams, front collision mitigation, lane departure warning, lane keeping assist, and multiple airbags including driver monitoring. Additional convenience comes from intelligent access with push-button start, keyless entry, a back-up camera, rear parking aid, Bluetooth connectivity, and WiFi hotspot capability. Steel wheels with all-season tires and a full-size spare tire with jack and tool kit complete this capable cargo van package.
