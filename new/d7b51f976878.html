Built to conquer the toughest jobs with confidence, the 2026 Ford Super Duty F-550 DRW XL Regular Cab Chassis-Cab combines power, durability, and advanced features tailored for demanding work environments.

Powered by a robust 7.3L V8 gasoline engine paired with a TorqShift 10-speed automatic transmission, this rear-wheel-drive powerhouse offers selectable drive modes including tow/haul and trail, ensuring optimal performance across varied terrains. Dual rear wheels provide enhanced stability and load capacity, while the locking limited-slip differential improves traction in challenging conditions. The chassis is equipped with 4-wheel disc brakes and ABS for reliable stopping power, complemented by traction control and tow hooks for added safety and utility.

Designed for professional upfitters, the F-550 features a comprehensive rear-view camera prep kit, complete with wiring and mounting accessories, simplifying integration of custom camera systems. The CNG/Propane gaseous engine prep package offers flexibility for alternative fuel conversions, catering to businesses seeking greener options.

Inside, the medium dark slate 40/20/40 cloth bench seat includes driver lumbar support and storage, providing comfort during long hauls. Heated mirrors with integrated turn signals enhance visibility and safety. Stainless steel wheel covers add a durable, professional finish. With its rugged build and thoughtful work-focused features, this Ford Super Duty F-550 is ready to tackle any heavy-duty task with ease.
