Experience the 2027 Ford Explorer ST-Line in Star White Metallic Tri-Coat, combining sporty design with advanced technology and versatile comfort for every journey.

Powered by a turbocharged 2.3L EcoBoost I-4 engine with auto start-stop technology and paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission, this 4x4 SUV delivers responsive performance and confident handling. The ST-Line Street Pack enhances its athletic stance with 21-inch Magnetite-painted aluminum wheels, P275/45R21 all-season tires, and striking red-painted front and rear brake calipers. A rear spoiler adds to the dynamic exterior profile, complemented by LED headlights and fog lamps for improved visibility.

Inside, the Onyx interior features heated unique cloth captain’s chairs with red stitching, offering 10-way power adjustment for the driver and 8-way for the passenger, both with lumbar support and seat memory for personalized comfort. The Premium Package adds ambient lighting, second-row heated seats, an auto-dimming interior rearview mirror, rain-sensing wipers, and power/heated glass mirrors with memory and LED turn signals.

Technology is front and centre with an aerial view display system, adaptive cruise control with steering assist, blind spot and cross-traffic alerts, and a back-up camera for safer driving. The panoramic opening Vista roof with power shade and remote control front windows enhance the cabin’s airy feel. SiriusXM with a 3-year subscription keeps entertainment seamless on the go. Tow hitch capability and a full suite of safety features, including lane keeping assist and front collision mitigation, make this Explorer ready for any adventure.
